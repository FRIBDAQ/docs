<a name="details"></a>## Detailed Description


[[DAQ::DDAS::DDASHitUnpacker|classDAQ_1_1DDAS_1_1DDASHitUnpacker]] is capable of unpacking raw hits from DDAS files. Typical trace analysis may involve fitting traces to one or two pulses. This class extends the [[DDASHitUnpacker|classDAQ_1_1DDAS_1_1DDASHitUnpacker]] class to support access to the results of the fit which have been tacked on the back end of a hit by CDDASAnalyzer.cpp.
